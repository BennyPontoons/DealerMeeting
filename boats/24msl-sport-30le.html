What's New for 2027

2027 updates

  • 30TH Anniversary Edition: The 30th Anniversary Limited Edition Package represents the pinnacle of Bennington's legacy, blending exclusive design elements with refined craftsmanship to create a truly collectible model. From the custom exterior styling and distinctive anniversary badging to the unique furniture color palette, upgraded table, branded underseat lining, and custom steering wheel, every detail has been thoughtfully curated to celebrate 30 years of premium boating. Paired with a signature headlight startup sequence, this special edition delivers an elevated ownership experience that is as memorable as the adventures ahead.
  • Powershade Bimini: The new PowerShade Bimini redifines onwater comfort with 10 feet of expansive shade coverage and effortless push-button operation. Designed to blend seamlessly into Bennington's premium styling, the PowerShade allows boaters to instantly adjust their environment without interupting the moment. The result is a more luxurious, comfortable, and enjoyable experience, helping passengers stay cool and relaxed from sunrise to sunset.
  • Stage 3 Audio Package Stage 3 Audio Package features a premium Rockford Fosgate sound system with a PMX6-BB source unit, six 6.5" RGB-capable speakers,PMX-R20B aft remote, and a PMPS-10 10" subwoofer/amplifier combination. The result is rich, immersive sound and customizable lighting that bring a luxury entertainment experience to every day on the water.
  • Sport and Luxe Trim Style Content Change: For MY27, we have replaced the M1 Lighted RGB Speakers with RGB Toe Kick Lighting as a standard feature within the M-Series Sport and Luxe Trims. This change alligns with out new MY27 audio Strategy and the introduction of our enhanced Rockford Fosgate Stage Audio offerings.
  • New Ski Tow Bar Ready for your next watersports adventure, Bennington's new reinforced ski tow bar is built to meet the latest industry towing standards for increased strength and durability. The upgrade design delivers added confidence when towing skiers and riders, while seamlessly integrating with the boat's premium fit and finish. More capibility, more confidence, and more fun for everyone on board.
