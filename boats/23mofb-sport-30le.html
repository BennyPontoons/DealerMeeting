What's New for 2027

2027 updates

  • Bennington 30th Anniversary Limited Edition: The 30th Anniversary Limited Edition Package represents the pinnacle of Bennington's legacy, blending exclusive design elements with refined craftsmanship to create a truly collectible model. From the custom exterior styling and distinctive anniversary badging to the unique furniture color palette, upgraded table, branded underseat lining, and custom steering wheel, every detail has been thoughtfully curated to celebrate 30 years of premium boating. Paired with a signature headlight startup sequence, this special edition delivers an elevated ownership experience that is as memorable as the adventures ahead.
  • Powershade Bimini: The new PowerShade Bimini redifines onwater comfort with 10 feet of expansive shade coverage and effortless push-button operation. Designed to blend seamlessly into Bennington's premium styling, the PowerShade allows boaters to instantly adjust their environment without interupting the moment. The result is a more luxurious, comfortable, and enjoyable experience, helping passengers stay cool and relaxed from sunrise to sunset.
  • Sport and Luxe Trim Style Content Change: For MY27, we have replaced the M1 Lighted RGB Speakers with RGB Toe Kick Lighting as a standard feature within the M-Series Sport and Luxe Trims. This change alligns with out new MY27 audio Strategy and the introduction of our enhanced Rockford Fosgate Stage Audio offerings.
  • Stage 1 Audio Package: Equipped with a Rockford Fosgate PMX-1 headunit and six RM speakers, Stage 1 Audio delivers crisp, premium sound throughout the boat. Designed as the starting point of Bennington's stage audio offerings, customers can upgrade to higher stages for even more power, deeper bass, and an immersive listening experience.
