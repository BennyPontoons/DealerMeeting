Where to Start

Four questions that pick your series.

Where do you boat?

If your're cruising inland lakes and neighborhood waterways, the S One offers an affordable way to get on the water. For larger lakes, longer days, and boaters who want more floorplans and performance LX Line delivers upgraded comfort and capibility. If your adventures include large water the QX Line is built without compromise.

How do you intend to use your boat?

For relaxing cruises and family outings, the S Series offers versatile layouts and excelent value. If entertaining is your priority, the M Series add premium ammentities and elevated comfort. For boater who loves watersports and performance the RX Line delivers sporting handling and exhilarating power.

Are you looking to stay in a specefic price range?

The S One provides premium Bennington quality at the most accessible price point. The M Series balances luxury and value with expanded features and technology. For customers seeking top-tier craftsmanship and premium appointments, the Q Series represents the pinnacle of luxury boating.

Do you want a customizable boat or standard features?

The S One simplifies the buying process witha well-equipped package and fewer decisions. The R Series is one of Bennington's most customizable platforms, offering extensive floorplans, features, and design options. For buyers who want the latest innovations, premium materials, and the ultimate ability to personalize their boat, the QX Line stands at the top of the lineup.
